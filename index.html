Galápagos history, knowledge and memory

Galapagensis

An independent research programme connecting the dispersed documentary history, memory and knowledge of the Galápagos Islands.

A distributed documentary corpus

Galápagos documentary heritage is dispersed among institutions and private collections throughout Ecuador and the world.

Much of it is publicly accessible but remains fragmented, poorly connected, unevenly described, and largely unexplored as a coherent historical corpus. Galapagensis identifies, documents, contextualizes, connects and researches these materials, while generating new historical knowledge and making the resulting research openly accessible.

The documented relationship

The dispersion of the evidence is not merely an inconvenience. It is itself part of the history Galapagensis can reveal.

The central intellectual unit is not only the digitized document. It is also the researched connection among documents, people, places, objects, expeditions, specimens, memories and repositories that may be scattered across the world.
